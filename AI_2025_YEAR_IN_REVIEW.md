# AI Year in Review: 2025 (Retrospective)

**Report Date:** April 21, 2026
**Status:** Compiled from Archival Records
**Operational Context:** March/April 2026

## 1. Major Model Releases & API Milestones

### OpenAI
*   **January 2025:** Released **Operator** (agentic browser) and **o3-mini** (reasoning model).
*   **February 2025:** Launched **GPT-4.5**, focusing on reduced hallucinations and improved instruction following.
*   **August 7, 2025:** Officially launched **GPT-5**. This release introduced native multimodality and integrated thinking modes, significantly improving reasoning performance. (Source: *TechRadar*, *Wow Labz*)
*   **December 11, 2025:** Introduced **GPT-5.2**, representing a jump in general intelligence (SWE-bench score: 80.0, AIME: 100%). (Source: *OpenAI Developer Community*)

### Google DeepMind
*   **January 2025:** Released **Gemini Flash Thinking 0121**.
*   **March 2025:** Launched **Gemini 2.5 Pro**, introducing advanced agentic capabilities and 3-hour video context.
*   **November 18, 2025:** Launched **Gemini 3 Pro** in public preview, featuring the `thinking_level` parameter. (Source: *Google Developers*, *Google Blog*)
*   **December 17, 2025:** Released **Gemini 3 Flash Preview**, optimizing Gemini 3 reasoning for low-latency tasks. (Source: *Google AI for Developers*)

### Anthropic
*   **February 24, 2025:** Launched **Claude 3.7 Sonnet** and **Claude Code**. Claude 3.7 Sonnet was the first "hybrid reasoning model" with visible thinking. (Source: *Anthropic News*)
*   **May 14, 2025:** Released updated **Responsible Scaling Policy (V2.2)**. (Source: *Anthropic RSP*)

### Meta
*   **April 2025:** Released **Llama 4** family (Scout 17B and Maverick MoE). (Source: *Reuters*, *Ollama*)

### xAI
*   **February 19, 2025:** Unveiled **Grok 3 Beta**, achieving high scores on AIME 2025. (Source: *x.ai/news*)
*   **July 9, 2025:** Launched **Grok 4**, featuring real-time search and tool use.
*   **November 17, 2025:** Released **Grok 4.1** and **Grok 4.1 Thinking**. (Source: *Wikipedia*)
